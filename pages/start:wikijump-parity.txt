A secondary verification record, with a different purpose from the theme table of the [[[start|Sigma-10 display-check top page]]]. The purpose of this wiki set is theme porting and the Sigma-10 migration, and this page is not a record of that work. Wikijump is software still under development, so some pages cannot reproduce the Wikidot rendering; that is expected. For the 153 existing articles on production SCP-JP, this page records whether what a reader sees on the Wikijump side matches the Wikidot side. Mismatches are collected here as Wikijump-side issues and are not counted as remaining porting or migration work.
[[/div]]
[[/div]]

**Measured build eaefd8bf27 (partly re-measured on f7e6cd103).** In the reproduction-state column, "match" means what the reader sees is identical between Wikidot and Wikijump, and "mismatch" means there is a difference, of the kind named in parentheses.

+ Categories of mismatch

Color differences (body and heading colors), literal differences (leaked markup-like text), image differences (broken images and loading differences), collapsible differences, tab differences, and include-error differences.
