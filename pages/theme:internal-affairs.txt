This is the Internal Affairs Theme by [[*user badooga]]. This [[[theme:jakstyle|JAKSTYLE]]]-derived theme is meant for works centering the Department of Internal Affairs, which is introduced in [[[SCP-9000]]] as an internal intelligence gathering group within the Foundation[[span class="fnnum"]].[[/span]][[span class="fncon"]]Though irrelevant to this theme, I personally envision Internal Affairs as having [[[fire-suppression-department-hub|Fire Suppression]]], Human Resources, and [[[Internal Affairs|Internal Security]]] as some of its subdivisions.[[/span]].
