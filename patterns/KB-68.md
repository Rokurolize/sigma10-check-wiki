# KB-68 — Candidate adopted from the production JP counterpart (provenance route undeclared)

- **Class:** C. Cross-cutting, not a defect
- **Detection cost:** cross-file diff
- **Provability:** FILE-PROVABLE
- **Status:** adopted
- **Basis:** static-diff
- **Dedupe key:** provenance route / candidate body inherited from the production JP counterpart rather than derived from the branch original / three-way similarity census (candidate vs production vs original)
- **Support:** 1 (static-diff)
- **Evidence source:** R/review-scout/TAXONOMY.md section 2, residue class R4 (documentation-pass taxonomy sample, 2026-11-17).

## Mechanism

Most candidates are derived from the branch original at the recorded
reference revision, and every instrument in this directory (follow-up
diffs, exhibit censuses, supplement arithmetic) assumes that
derivation. Some are not: the porter adopted the live JP production
version's body as the candidate's base, because production already
embodies years of JP-side drift (literal values where the original
later moved to var() custom properties, older selector forms,
JP-only edits). Adoption is a legitimate porting route, often the
safest one. The hazard is purely in the reading: a diff of an adopted
candidate against the branch original measures production drift and
reports it as port change, and the T4 follow-up diff inherits the
same confusion in reverse. If the provenance route is not declared,
reviewers misattribute drift to the port and port changes to drift,
and neither error is visible from the diff itself.

## Static detection

Three-way similarity census over the population that has a production
counterpart (pages-existing/): for each theme, compute the
candidate's similarity to (a) its production counterpart and (b) its
branch original at the reference revision. Adoption signature:
candidate-to-production similarity at or near identity (index
instance 0.964) while the candidate-to-original diff is large and
dominated by production-drift markers (var() in the original against
literal hex in the candidate, older selector forms, JP-only blocks
with no original counterpart). The census runs on repo files only;
both CSS channels are parsed per the two-channel rule before any
similarity figure is quoted.

## Confirmed instances

penumbra: theme:penumbra-jp is 96.4% identical to
pages-existing/existing:theme-penumbra.txt, while its diff against
the EN original is dominated by production drift (taxonomy sample:
123 of its 231 change blocks were residue under original-derived
classification, about 110 of them this shape). The census has not
yet been run over the remaining production-counterpart rows;
penumbra is the sole confirmed instance, and the recipe above is the
instrument for completing the set.

## False positives and disposition

- T10 remote-original layering (animal-instead-of-beast,
  path-of-jinin) is a different mechanism: the candidate layers the
  live original by URL at render time; nothing is adopted into the
  candidate body.
- Minimal themes saturate any similarity measure (a 30-line shell is
  near-identical to everything); require the drift-marker leg of the
  signature, not similarity alone.
- JP originals (min-theme, shushuin) have no branch original; their
  provenance declaration is the no-reference-revision form, not this
  pattern.

## Fix

No candidate edit. The theme's explanation declares the provenance
route in its header (adopted from the production JP counterpart,
with the counterpart's fullname), and its diffs are read
production-first: candidate against production for what the port
changed, production against original for the drift the port
inherited. Sweeps record the route beside the theme so
original-derived recipes skip or re-base the row instead of charging
drift.
