# KB-09 — Duplicate bare `[[module Rate]]` beside the rate-bearing preview credit module

- **Class:** A. Theme defect, file-provable
- **Detection cost:** pure grep
- **Provability:** FILE-PROVABLE
- **Status:** adopted
- **Basis:** static-diff
- **Dedupe key:** rating furniture / live bare [[module Rate]] beside the rate-bearing credit:start-preview, unlabelled / both-present test with labelling classification
- **Support:** 1 (static-diff)
- **Evidence source:** R/KNOWLEDGE-SEED-INVENTORY.md (compiled 2026-11-17 from the GOSIGN2 review reports and coordinator adjudications named there).

## Mechanism

`credit:start-preview` already embeds a rating widget. A candidate that also
carries a live bare `[[module Rate]]` outside any exhibit renders two rating
widgets on the page today. The porting error is mechanical: the original's bare
Rate was dropped-or-kept without noticing the preview module replaced it.

## Static detection

In the candidate, test for both (a) `credit:start-preview` present and (b) a
live `[[module Rate]]` / `[[module rate]]` outside `@@…@@` escapes, outside
tag-gated blocks, and outside a labelled exhibit pair. Both together = defect.
(Careful: the regex `credit:start\b` also matches `credit:start-preview`; count
module flavours precisely, per AGENTS.md section 8.)

## Confirmed instances

al-slop (live Rate at L13-15), extra-black-highlighter-theme (bare Rate L60),
flopstyle (bare Rate L13): REPORT-GOSIGN2-LOCALIZE-A rows 2, 10, 11 (pattern 3).
Checker `double-rate` info is this same shape.

## False positives and disposition

A deliberately LABELLED, paired exhibit stays (precedents: classic, monotypical,
flopstyle-dark, shark-punching-centre-bhl's 評価：/クレジットモジュール付き評価： pair). Escaped
or tag-gated hits are false positives. Judge by labelling, not by the hit
(AGENTS.md section 5 Rate rule).

## Fix

Delete the bare Rate; keep the preview module. Where no credit module exists at
all, see KB-10.

## Sweep amendment (2026-11-17, SWEEP-A1)

All three confirmed instances are marked FIXED: al-slop, extra-black-highlighter-theme and flopstyle now carry the preview credit include and zero live bare [[module Rate]] occurrences. ## Amendment (2026-11-17, FIX5-FURNA adjudication): the original-shipped format-specimen Rate

The sweep amendment above states that every both-present case in the
post-fix population is a labelled exhibit after context
classification. That is too strong: saphir is the counterexample, and
the ledger charged it in class B on exactly that gap. FIX5-FURNA
dispositioned it on a different ground, recorded here as a third
false-positive shape beside labelled pairs and escaped or tag-gated
hits: a live, unlabelled [[module Rate]] inside a documentation or
format-example section of the page (saphir: the `+ GoIフォーマット例`
block, candidate L205-210, 186 lines from the credit module at L24),
where all three of these hold: (i) the original at the reference
revision carries the identical Rate in the identical example block
(EN original rev 14, L211, inside its `+ Example GOI Format` block);
(ii) the original's own format documentation confirms the Rate is an
intentional part of the specimen (orig L228: the format is pasted
before or after the rating module); (iii) the candidate's primary
Rate position was correctly ported to credit:start-preview, so the
port preserves the original's rating-widget count and introduces no
extra widget. A bare Rate failing any one condition charges as
before. Disposition: by-design exhibit; the checker's double-rate
info stands, dispositioned. The deciding evidence is the original
comparison, not labelling: judge specimen Rates against the original
before charging.
