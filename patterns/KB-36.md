# KB-36 — WebKit stray "S" glyph for the CC badge

- **Class:** C. Cross-cutting, not a defect
- **Detection cost:** pure grep
- **Provability:** FILE-PROVABLE
- **Status:** adopted
- **Basis:** measured-render
- **Dedupe key:** CC badge / WebKit-only stray S glyph confined to the badge / engine-plus-location signature in captures
- **Support:** 1 (measured-render)
- **Evidence source:** R/KNOWLEDGE-SEED-INVENTORY.md (compiled 2026-11-17 from the GOSIGN2 review reports and coordinator adjudications named there).

## Mechanism

WebKit renders a stray "S" glyph where the credit modal / section-30 replica
shows the CC badge: an engine quirk in how WebKit paints the badge's icon font
or ligature, identical across themes, absent in Chromium.

## Static detection

Disposition by engine and location: a stray glyph confined to the CC badge,
WebKit-only, is this quirk. No candidate file content predicts or fixes it.

## Confirmed instances

AGENTS.md section 3 T2 environment-layer lines; RERUN16 notes (old-money page:
WebKit "S" CC badge seen once; shark-punching-centre-bhl sample: WebKit stray
glyph cross-cutting).

## False positives and disposition

A stray glyph anywhere else, or in both engines, is not this pattern and needs
its own reading.

## Fix

None.
