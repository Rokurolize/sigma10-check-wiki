# KB-13 — Global `.modalbox` fixed centering pins the static credit replica at viewport centre (space class)

- **Class:** A. Theme defect, file-provable
- **Detection cost:** pure grep
- **Provability:** FILE-PROVABLE
- **Evidence source:** R/KNOWLEDGE-SEED-INVENTORY.md (compiled 2026-11-17 from the GOSIGN2 review reports and coordinator adjudications named there).

## Mechanism

Display-check samples embed a static replica of the credit modal
(`.credit-static-view .modalbox`) that must render in-flow at its section. A
theme whose global `.modalbox` rule sets `position: fixed; left: 50%; top: 50%;
transform: translate(-50%,-50%)` (styling the REAL modal) also captures the
replica, because the master sample only overrides the replica's container, not
the box. The replica then floats pinned at viewport centre over the header on
every load, in both engines.

## Static detection

Grep the candidate's CSS for a global (non-scoped) `.modalbox` rule with
`position: fixed` and centering transforms, and check for the absence of a
`.credit-static-view .modalbox` carve-out resetting position/transform. Both
together = defect predicted with certainty; a live-DOM probe of the computed
style on the sample confirms in seconds.

## Confirmed instances

space: replica computes to `visibility: visible; position: fixed; top: 384px;
left: 683px` on the sample, covering the wordmark; the same replica on the
Sigma-9 sample computes `position: relative` in-flow: REPORT-GOSIGN2-RENDER-S2
reports (live-DOM probe evidence).

## False positives and disposition

A `.modalbox` fixed rule WITH the `.credit-static-view` carve-out is the fixed
form, not a finding. Page-side credit-modal overflow on real-module pages is the
distinct KB-31.

## Fix

Add the `.credit-static-view .modalbox` carve-out mirroring the existing
`.modalcontainer` override (`position: relative; top: auto; left: auto;
transform: none`).
