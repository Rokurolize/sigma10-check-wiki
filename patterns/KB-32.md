# KB-32 — Credit-modal error on preview-module pages (preview-form)

- **Class:** C. Cross-cutting, not a defect
- **Detection cost:** pure grep
- **Provability:** FILE-PROVABLE
- **Evidence source:** R/KNOWLEDGE-SEED-INVENTORY.md (compiled 2026-11-17 from the GOSIGN2 review reports and coordinator adjudications named there).

## Mechanism

Candidate and display-check pages use the PREVIEW credit module
(`credit:start-preview`); the test wiki replaces it with a preview notice, and
the preview form has no modal-opening mechanism at all. render_check's
credit-button click therefore reports `credit-modal` on essentially every theme,
uniformly. It is a property of the preview form, not of any theme's CSS.

## Static detection

File-provable disposition: if the page under check includes
`credit:start-preview` / `credit:end-preview`, every `credit-modal` line for
that page is preview-form. No file check on the theme is needed or meaningful.
Judge real modal behaviour only on real-module pages (KB-31); on preview pages
verify the credit block's static rendering by screenshot.

## Confirmed instances

Universal across the render reports (S2-A X4 and every lane's separation notes);
the ruling is recorded in GOSIGN-TESTS.md T2 (WSL copy, amended 2026-11-16) and
AGENTS.md section 3. wish-for-miracle and starbasalt samples carry NO
credit-modal machine line, which is recorded as-is and does not change the
disposition (RENDER-ROWS-G).

## False positives and disposition

Never a fix target, never charge to a theme, never modify checker scripts to
force the modal. The information-transfer failure this caused (lanes initially
charging it) is why the recipe is file-first: check the credit module flavour
before reading any credit-modal line.

## Fix

None.
