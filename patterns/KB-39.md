- **Class:** C. Cross-cutting, not a defect
- **Detection cost:** cross-file diff
- **Provability:** FILE-PROVABLE
- **Status:** adopted

- **Support:** 1 (measured-render)
- **Evidence source:** R/KNOWLEDGE-SEED-INVENTORY.md (compiled 2026-11-17 from the GOSIGN2 review reports and coordinator adjudications named there).

## Mechanism

render_check's contrast warn fires on computed pairs that ignore layering,
translucency, and design intent: readable pairs get flagged (dark text on light
blue measured as a band average), and deliberate redaction (contrast 1) is
flagged as failure. The warn is a nomination, not a measurement of readability.

## Static detection

Never charge or clear on the warn alone. Pair this with KB-28's recipe: compute
the actual composed foreground/background pair and check design intent in the
original before treating any contrast line as evidence either way.

## Confirmed instances

normal page: contrast warn 1 for the band is a false positive (dark text on
light blue is readable): RERUN16. cosmonaut: contrast 3.2 readable, machine warn
only: RENDER-ROWS-1. halloween: contrast 1 is redaction by design: RENDER-B row
14.

## False positives and disposition

This entry IS the false-positive record for contrast lines; real unreadable
pairs are KB-28, which requires the screenshot or computed-style evidence.

None.
