# KB-60 — Production license-box form uncertainty (do not convert forms)

- **Class:** F. Named exception / standing disposition
- **Detection cost:** pure grep
- **Provability:** FILE-PROVABLE
- **Evidence source:** R/KNOWLEDGE-SEED-INVENTORY.md (compiled 2026-11-17 from the GOSIGN2 review reports and coordinator adjudications named there).

## Mechanism

Candidates variously include `:scp-jp:component:license-box`,
`:scp-jp:component:license-box-backend`, and the campaign's own port
`component:license-box-theme-jp`. Campaign records conflict on which production
pages actually exist: FIX2-INCLUDE (suprematism) and FIX3-FD (flopstyle-dark)
used `license-box` as a verified role-equivalent, while FIX3-S2EN cites 404
evidence for it and used `license-box-backend` for derby.

## Static detection

Record whichever form a candidate carries, verbatim; do NOT "fix" one form into
another without fresh probe evidence (AGENTS.md sections 5 and 9, open
contradiction). A fresh fullname probe on production scp-jp is the only
acceptable new evidence.

## Confirmed instances

AGENTS.md section 9 (production license-box existence); FIX2-INCLUDE, FIX3-FD,
FIX3-S2EN as cited there.

## False positives and disposition

Open contradiction; per-theme repoints already present stand as recorded.
