# KB-58 — Deferred asset 404s (draft-bucket hosting state)

- **Class:** F. Named exception / standing disposition
- **Detection cost:** needs live probe
- **Provability:** FILE-PROVABLE
- **Evidence source:** R/KNOWLEDGE-SEED-INVENTORY.md (compiled 2026-11-17 from the GOSIGN2 review reports and coordinator adjudications named there).

## Mechanism

Campaign rule: no asset re-uploads to the check wikis. Candidate and sample
pages therefore render without their art (logos, headers, decorative images 404
or ORB) until posting, when assets are re-hosted to SCP-JP storage. The 404 is
the deferred hosting state of the whole campaign, not a per-theme defect and not
a localization defect.

## Static detection

A 404/ORB on a decorative asset whose canonical reference points at draft-bucket
or original-side storage slated for posting re-host is this disposition.
Distinguish from KB-02 (the URL serves NOWHERE, including its real home) by the
KB-02 probe protocol: deferred assets have a known live home or a posting
re-host path.

## Confirmed instances

AGENTS.md section 6 (draft-bucket asset 404s; campaign-wide deferred condition).
Per-theme instances across all render reports (old-money OldMoney_Logo.png,
saphir headers, gsk site-logo.png broken alt-text header, magicity
magicity.svg/shujia.png, black-rain logo).

## False positives and disposition

Standing disposition, owner-deferred. Never charge, never re-upload inside the
campaign.
