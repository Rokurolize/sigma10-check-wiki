# KB-35 — Site-header subtitle doubling at ≤375px

- **Class:** C. Cross-cutting, not a defect
- **Detection cost:** pure grep
- **Provability:** FILE-PROVABLE
- **Evidence source:** R/KNOWLEDGE-SEED-INVENTORY.md (compiled 2026-11-17 from the GOSIGN2 review reports and coordinator adjudications named there).

## Mechanism

At phone widths the site header shows the subtitle twice. The majority
attribution is the Sigma-10 site theme's own header construction
(environment-layer): the doubling appears across unrelated themes at the same
widths, driven by the site CSS, not by any candidate's rules.

## Static detection

Disposition: do not charge a theme for subtitle doubling seen at ≤375 unless the
theme is night-rush (see the open attribution). No candidate-file check supports
charging any other theme.

## Confirmed instances

Environment attribution: RENDER-D, RENDER-S2-A (X1), S2-B, S2-H. Theme-specific
attribution for night-rush-theme: RENDER-C row 24 ("not a wiki-wide artifact;
much-cool and other themes show a normal header at the same widths").

## False positives and disposition

OPEN CONTRADICTION (AGENTS.md section 9): the night-rush attribution is not
reconciled with the majority environment disposition. Record per-theme, flag for
the coordinator, do not invent a resolution.

## Fix

None at theme level under the majority disposition.
