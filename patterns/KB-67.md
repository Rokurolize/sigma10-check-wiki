# KB-67 — Documentation or sample exhibit dropped or replaced without a recorded ground

- **Class:** A. Theme defect, file-provable
- **Detection cost:** cross-file diff
- **Provability:** FILE-PROVABLE
- **Status:** adopted
- **Basis:** static-diff, lane-report
- **Dedupe key:** exhibit census / original usage or sample exhibit absent or replaced in the candidate with no ground recorded / exhibit census diff plus explanation-ground check
- **Support:** 2 (static-diff, lane-report)
- **Evidence source:** R/review-scout/TAXONOMY.md section 2, residue class R3 (documentation-pass taxonomy sample, 2026-11-17); the live-structure form charged as R/GOSIGN2-LEDGER.md section (e) class I, repaired in FIX5-STRUCTA and FIX5-STRUCTB.

## Mechanism

A branch original teaches its own use with exhibits: tabview/tab
demonstrations, [[code]] samples, sample articles, and worked
examples built on components. A port can translate those exhibits in
place, replace them with JP-valid equivalents, or drop them. The
first two are porting. The third, done silently, is indistinguishable
in the candidate from a structural deletion, and the reader of the
posted theme loses the documentation the original shipped. Ledger
class I charged exactly this shape when it reached live structure
(emptied preset blocks, dropped showcases); this pattern is the same
shape at the documentation layer. The defect is not the drop itself
but the absence of a recorded ground: with no ground on record, no
reviewer can tell a sanctioned replacement from content lost in the
port.

## Static detection

Exhibit census, candidate against the original at the reference
revision, both files parsed in full (exhibits live in the wikitext
body and in [[code]] blocks; where an exhibit is CSS, both CSS
channels are parsed per the two-channel rule, and the resolved base
is subtracted before an absence is claimed). Enumerate exhibit units
in the original: [[tabview]]/[[tab]] blocks, [[code]] exhibit blocks,
sample or exhibit sections, and the sample includes they carry (e.g.
foreign-component sample includes). For each unit, classify the
candidate side: present and translated; present and replaced
(different exhibit, same teaching role); absent. Every absent or
replaced unit is then checked against the record: a ground in the
theme's explanation (Structure section), a ledger disposition, or a
named exception in this directory. An absent or replaced unit with
no ground on record is the hit.

## Confirmed instances

Confirmed instances of the restructuring shape (documentation-pass
taxonomy sample, 14 themes read block by block): starbasalt (the
original's Chinese tabview exhibit deleted in the candidate);
andesite (sample articles built on :fendsfox:component:cobs exhibits
deleted); suprematism (the original's :root code exhibits
restructured rather than translated in place). The sample records
the shape and the themes; the per-theme disposition (sanctioned
replacement vs silent drop) is adjudicated theme by theme against
the record, and an instance becomes a charge only when no ground is
found.

## False positives and disposition

- Labelled paired exhibits kept deliberately (the AGENTS.md section 5
  Rate-exhibit rule) are KB-09 / KB-59 shapes, not this pattern.
- Deliberate specimen shapes recorded as such are KB-37; the peroxide
  escaped-exhibit family is KB-56.
- An exhibit demonstrating a component with no JP counterpart, dropped
  with the ground recorded (the KB-54 named-exception route), is
  dispositioned, not charged: the recording is the difference.
- Exhibits inside @@...@@ escapes that document syntax are text,
  judged under KB-18 / P10, not census units.

## Fix

Restore the exhibit translated in place; or, where the original
exhibit cannot work on JP (foreign component, dead delivery), replace
it with a JP-valid exhibit teaching the same use and record the
ground in the explanation's Structure section. A drop with neither
restoration nor a recorded ground is charged; where the dropped
exhibit was live structure rather than documentation, it is ledger
class I and follows that wave's repair.
