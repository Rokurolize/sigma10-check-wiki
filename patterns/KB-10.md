# KB-10 — Credit furniture missing or incomplete (no-credit, no-rate, credit-missing-fields)

- **Class:** A. Theme defect, file-provable
- **Detection cost:** pure grep
- **Provability:** FILE-PROVABLE
- **Evidence source:** R/KNOWLEDGE-SEED-INVENTORY.md (compiled 2026-11-17 from the GOSIGN2 review reports and coordinator adjudications named there).

## Mechanism

JP posting requires the credit block with the eight TASL-derived fields (タイトル,
原題, 著作権者, 作成年, 翻訳責任者, 翻訳年, 初訳時参照リビジョン, 元記事リンク). Candidates with no credit module
at all are also unratable (no Rate anywhere), and skeleton credits (2 of 8
fields) drop the original's attribution, including font/image credits.

## Static detection

Run the staff checker and read codes `no-credit`, `no-rate`,
`credit-missing-fields`; or grep the candidate for `credit:start` and count the
eight field labels in the credit block. Field values must be evidenced, never
invented.

## Confirmed instances

blankstyle (no credit module at all, every field missing, theme unratable):
LOCALIZE-A row 6. dear-dictator (2-field skeleton, original attribution
dropped): LOCALIZE-A row 9. black-highlighter-theme (元記事リンク absent, three fields
missing, label variance): LOCALIZE-A row 5. al-slop (6 of 8 missing): LOCALIZE-A
row 2. Fixed across FIX-CREDIT-A/B/C/D and FIX2-CREDIT waves.

## False positives and disposition

Created component pages deliberately leave `no-credit` (AGENTS.md sections 5-6);
this pattern is for theme candidates. The preview-vs-plain form itself is left
alone where out of scope (pataphysics keeps `credit:start`, warn only).

## Fix

Add `credit:start-preview` (it is rate-bearing) and only the missing fields,
values evidenced.
