# KB-63 — JP license box dropped by a candidate whose original carried one

- **Class:** A. Theme defect, file-provable
- **Detection cost:** cross-file diff
- **Provability:** FILE-PROVABLE
- **Evidence source:** sigma10-review-20261117 findings/A13.md, finding A13-1 (full-population include scan of all 105 candidate/original pairs).

## Mechanism

Carrying a license box is the established JP supplement: 54 of 105
candidates include the JP form (`:scp-jp:component:license-box-backend`
plus `:scp-jp:component:license-box-end`), including 44 whose originals
had no license box at all (exemplar: theme:fish-jp.txt lines 234, 252).
Of the 15 originals that carry the EN `license-box-theme`, 8 candidates
kept a license box in JP form and 7 dropped every license-box include,
so the candidate renders without the license furniture its original
(and its production JP counterpart surface) carries.

## Static detection

For each candidate/original pair: grep the original for `license-box`;
if present, grep the candidate (full text, both channels) for
`license-box`. Original carries one and candidate has zero matches is
the defect. Separately record which license-box form a candidate
carries, verbatim, under KB-60 (forms are not converted without fresh
probe evidence). Note: the repo's `component:license-box-theme-jp` is
used by no candidate at all, which connects to the open license-box
question in AGENTS.md section 9.

## Confirmed instances

alcur-theme-jp, art-deco-jp, caretaker-sigma-jp, fuladh-theme-jp,
internal-affairs-jp, janitor-closet-jp, oceanbound-jp (A13-1; e.g.
theme:art-deco.txt lines 850, 852 carry the EN includes while
`grep -c license-box pages/theme:art-deco-jp.txt` is 0). All seven
rows read complete on /start.

## False positives and disposition

A candidate whose original carried no license box is not charged under
this pattern (the 44 additions are the supplement norm, not a per-theme
obligation proven pair by pair). internal-affairs is also on the KB-11
watch list; the two charges are distinct mechanisms and both stand.

## Fix

Add the standard `:scp-jp:component:license-box-backend` include (with
the author parameter from the original's `license-box-theme` call
where present, e.g. internal-affairs' `author=badooga`) and the
`license-box-end` include to the 7 candidates, matching the sibling
form; or, if the license box is judged documentation-only, record that
decision once and remove it from the 54 instead. Do not leave the
split state (A13-1 proposed fix).
