# KB-33 — Static credit replica fixed-width overflow on samples (337-419px and 884px shapes)

- **Class:** C. Cross-cutting, not a defect
- **Detection cost:** cross-file diff
- **Provability:** FILE-PROVABLE
- **Status:** adopted
- **Basis:** measured-render, static-diff
- **Dedupe key:** sample page-overflow / measured widths in the static replica bands 337-419px or 884px / band-shape disposition, BHL samples excepted pending attribution
- **Support:** 2 (measured-render, static-diff)
- **Evidence source:** R/KNOWLEDGE-SEED-INVENTORY.md (compiled 2026-11-17 from the GOSIGN2 review reports and coordinator adjudications named there).

## Mechanism

Every display-check sample embeds the same static credit replica, a fixed-width
block. At 320/375 viewports the sample page's measured width becomes 337-419px,
and at 768 it becomes 884px, regardless of the theme. The numbers are the
replica's width signature, not the theme's layout.

## Static detection

Disposition by shape: a SAMPLE-page `page-overflow` whose measured widths fall
in the 337/389 (Chromium) or 416/419 (WebKit) bands at 320/375, or 884px at 768,
and whose candidate page does not show theme-own overflow beyond them, is
cross-cutting. The replica's fixed width can be read in the shared sample source
once and reused for every theme.

## Confirmed instances

Recorded as cross-cutting in RENDER-ROWS-1, RENDER-ROWS-2, RENDER-ROWS-G and
RERUN16 for dozens of themes (e.g. ared sample 884px@768; deserted-years-theme
sample 337/389 + 884 shapes).

## False positives and disposition

OPEN ATTRIBUTION (AGENTS.md section 9): for BHL-derived samples (bedbugs,
black-serpent, caretaker-theme, cleaning-services, clock-tower in RENDER-S2-A)
the same 337-419 numbers may be candidate-inherent BHL min-width overflow
(KB-23). Per-theme attribution is unresolved; do not auto-clear BHL-family
samples on shape alone.

## Fix

None (sample construction).
