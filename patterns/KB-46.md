# KB-46 — Screenshot ceiling incompleteness (32,767px cap, Chromium white-out)

- **Class:** E. Capture and measurement artefact, not a result
- **Detection cost:** pure grep
- **Provability:** FILE-PROVABLE
- **Evidence source:** R/KNOWLEDGE-SEED-INVENTORY.md (compiled 2026-11-17 from the GOSIGN2 review reports and coordinator adjudications named there).

## Mechanism

Sample pages run 20,000-31,000px tall. Playwright's full-page screenshot cap
(32,767px; Chromium also whites out past 16,384px in some paths) silently drops
or truncates `*-full.png` captures, most often WebKit full screenshots. A
missing screenshot is a capture gap, not a rendering result, and any T3 claim
resting on the missing image is unsupported.

## Static detection

Inventory the capture set per target: missing `*-full.png` (especially webkit
375/320 and webkit full sets) marks the run incomplete for T3 purposes; use
credit/sidebar crops and other viewports' captures, labelled as such. Never open
a raw full.png to check (slice it with pillow via uv).

## Confirmed instances

ordopharm-sample (all webkit *-full.png missing), magicity and normal samples
(webkit 375/320 full missing), shancheng/share-future-community samples
(over-ceiling WebKit full-page screenshots skipped in the full-engine re-run):
RERUN16 and RENDER-ROWS-G.

## False positives and disposition

A missing `*-sidebar.png` where the full capture SHOWS no control is KB-25
evidence, not this gap; the distinction is whether the relevant surface was
captured at all.

## Fix

None; record the gap and judge from the captures that exist.
