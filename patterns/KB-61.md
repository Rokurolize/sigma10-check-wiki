## Mechanism

monotypical's normalize need is served by an appended fragment in
`theme:basalt-jp` (its last code block, /7): a 12-rule trimmed subset of
monotypical's own /5, accepted on a "role, not bytes" reading after the brief's
premise (a complete normalize.css v8.0.1 inline copy) was disproved in-lane: the
cached CDN `normalize-min.css` is actually a Sigma-10 "unset" reset, not
normalize.css.

## Static detection

When checking basalt-family normalize coverage, compare ROLES (which elements
the fragment resets) rather than byte-diffing against normalize.css v8.0.1; a
byte-completeness charge requires a separate coordinator adjudication that does
not exist.
