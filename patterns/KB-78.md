# KB-78 — Empty collapsible beside a duplicated heading

- **Class:** A. Theme defect, file-provable
- **Detection cost:** pure grep
- **Provability:** FILE-PROVABLE
- **Status:** adopted
- **Basis:** static-diff
- **Dedupe key:** empty collapsible / a [[collapsible]] whose open is immediately followed by its close, typically beside a duplicated heading / open-close adjacency census
- **Support:** 2 (static-diff)
- **Evidence source:** R/review-scout/BATCH-RFIX11.md proposal 3, BATCH-RFIX7.md proposal 2, BATCH-RFIX11.md (scpdeclassified candidate L138-141).

## Mechanism

A structural relocation leaves a `[[collapsible]]` with an empty body
(open immediately followed by close), often beside a duplicated heading.
It is reader-visible dead furniture: the reader sees a heading and a
collapsible control with nothing inside. The classifier lands it in
restructuring residue and no signature counts it.

## Static detection

Grep candidates for a `[[collapsible]]` open token immediately followed
(by whitespace only) by its close token; count such pairs per file and
record their proximity to duplicated headings. A candidate-only empty
collapsible where the original's corresponding collapsible had content
is the hit.

## Confirmed instances

scpdeclassified (candidate L138-141, empty collapsible beside a duplicated
heading), and the three packages of the R-fix-7 batch whose original
display collapsible was left empty when its code block relocated.

## False positives and disposition

A deliberately empty collapsible with a recorded ground (a placeholder
for posting-time content) is dispositioned, not charged. An escaped
example in documentation is text, judged under KB-18, not a census unit.

## Fix

Restore the collapsible's body from the original, or remove the empty
collapsible and its duplicated heading. Record which was done in the
explanation's Structure section.
