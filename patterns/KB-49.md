# KB-49 — Stale static-cache phantom rendering

- **Class:** E. Capture and measurement artefact, not a result
- **Detection cost:** needs live probe

- **Status:** adopted
- **Basis:** lane-report

- **Support:** 1 (lane-report)
- **Evidence source:** R/KNOWLEDGE-SEED-INVENTORY.md (compiled 2026-11-17 from the GOSIGN2 review reports and coordinator adjudications named there).

## Mechanism

Wikidot serves compiled page/CSS from a static cache (`x-wikidot-static-cache`)
that can lag the canonical source: a page renders content its current source
forbids, or fails to render content its source contains (missing bibliography
targets, vanished blocks). Any verdict taken from the stale capture is a verdict
about the cache, not the theme.

## Static detection

When a rendering contradicts the canonical source, re-fetch the page's
`local--code` delivery with `?nocache=` and byte-compare against
`pages/<fullname>.txt` before dispositioning. A mismatch proves staleness; a
match moves the investigation back to the source.

## Confirmed instances

inkblot blank body recorded as possible stale-cache, not counted as a defect,
re-check after purge recommended (RENDER-B row 16). saphir sample section 27
"Bibliography item … not found" noted as possible stale static-cache /
page-state, not a theme defect (RERUN16). AGENTS.md section 9 records the
caution level for this mechanism as an open contradiction against KB-48's proven
form.

## False positives and disposition

Do not use staleness as a generic excuse: the `?nocache=` byte comparison is the
evidence, and without it the rendering stands.

## Fix

Purge/re-save and re-check; no candidate edit.
