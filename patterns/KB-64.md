# KB-64 — Bare component:sigma-plus include bypassing the JP port

- **Class:** A. Theme defect, file-provable
- **Detection cost:** pure grep
- **Provability:** FILE-PROVABLE
- **Evidence source:** sigma10-review-20261117 findings/A13.md, finding A13-2; FIX3-SP precedent in the GOSIGN2 campaign record.

## Mechanism

The JP port of the Sigma+ component exists in the repo as
`component:sigma-plus-jp`, and 6 candidates use it (art-deco-jp line 1,
black-diamond-jp, chaos-jp, derby-jp, fandom-jp, fossil-jp). Five
candidates instead include the bare `component:sigma-plus`. No
`pages/component:sigma-plus.txt` exists in the canonical repo, so
within the repo the bare include is unresolvable and, on the check
wiki, loads whatever non-port copy exists there rather than the JP
port, bypassing the port's localization entirely.

This is recorded as its own pattern rather than merged into KB-15:
KB-15 covers foreign-wiki includes kept where a verified JP
counterpart exists. Here the target is not foreign; it is the
unlocalized component's own name, the repo holds no file for it at
all, and the correct form is campaign-specific
(`component:sigma-plus-jp` on the check wikis;
`:scp-jp:component:sigma-plus-jp` at posting). The FIX3-SP fix wave
already settled that form.

## Static detection

Grep every candidate for `[[include component:sigma-plus]]` (the bare
form, without the `-jp` suffix). Each hit is the defect; count include
lines, not just files.

## Confirmed instances

lampeter-warm-jp (line 1), linear-black-jp, shancheng-jp,
share-future-community-jp, ultimate-reader-jp: 5 candidates, 7 include
lines (shancheng-jp and share-future-community-jp each include it
twice). share-future-community is also a confirmed KB-11 instance.

## False positives and disposition

The prefixed posting form and the `-jp` check-wiki form are correct
and are not charged. Escaped documentation examples (`@@...@@`) are
exempt, as elsewhere.

## Fix

Switch the 5 candidates (7 include lines) to
`component:sigma-plus-jp`, deduplicating the doubled includes in
shancheng-jp and share-future-community-jp (A13-2 proposed fix;
FIX3-SP precedent).
