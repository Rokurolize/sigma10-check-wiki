## Mechanism

candela imports the CN `fragment:parallel` set granularly, per-fragment, because
its accent palette depends on the granular cascade order. The JP
`theme:parallel-jp` offers a single bundle; importing the bundle alone reorders
the cascade and changes candela's accent palette, a display regression proven by
cascade simulation. The granular CN fragments serve correctly today.

## Static detection

Detect candela's granular `fragment:parallel` import lines and record this
exception; do NOT nominate them under KB-14/KB-15, and never restructure
`theme:parallel-jp` for a dependent. The FIX3-PC form (bundle import plus a
trailing `:root` override block after all @imports) is the recorded alternative
if the exception is ever revisited.
