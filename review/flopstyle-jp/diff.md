-# [[span class="keycap"]]authorbox[[/span]]
-# [[span class="keycap"]]sidebox[[/span]]
-
-Usage instructions vary depending on the type.
-
-* The [[span class="keycap"]]authorbox[[/span]] does not display on screens below [[span class="keycap"]]width=1200px[[/span]], which includes mobile screens. It can be set up to display page and author information.
-* The [[span class="keycap"]]sidebox[[/span]] collapses into the page edge on screens below [[span class="keycap"]]width=1200px[[/span]]. On those screens, it can be accessed by tapping your finger or hovering your cursor over it. It can be set up to display additional narrative information.
-
-Please consider these conditions before setting up these items -- a large percentage of SCP Wiki users browse via mobile devices.
-----
-++++* 1. Authorbox
-+++++* (See Top Left)
-----
-By default, the [[span class="keycap"]]authorbox[[/span]] is **__opt-out__**. It comes preloaded on all pages that include Flopstyle: LITE. If you wish to //remove// it, please replace this:

-
-[[div class="code terminal centered"]]

-[[/div]]

-By default, the [[span class="keycap"]]authorbox[[/span]] makes use of ListPages to automate the metadata displayed. You could theoretically copy the ListPages portion of the [[span class="keycap"]]authorbox[[/span]] onto //any// Wiki page, and these fields would populate in a way unique to that page. You may, of course, change any of these fields as desired.
-
-You may also edit your custom [[span class="keycap"]]authorbox[[/span]] to display flavor text tailored to your page, such as a note to readers.
-
-See the left side of the page for an [[span class="keycap"]]authorbox[[/span]] example.

-[[span class="keycap"]]flavorbox[[/span]] is the only div you should use inside an [[span class="keycap"]]authorbox[[/span]].

-
-If you have a page that does this, I recommend //against// inserting any [[span class="keycap"]]sidebox[[/span]]es in that same page, or it will prove rather messy.
-
-If you are planning on inserting any [[span class="keycap"]]sidecontainer[[/span]]s onto your page, I strongly recommend using the default [[span class="keycap"]]authorbox[[/span]]. The reason being, when a right-hand [[span class="keycap"]]authorbox[[/span]] and [[span class="keycap"]]sidecontainer[[/span]] are used in tandem, they will overlap and become visually messy. This has the potential to be very confusing for readers. So, it's better staying on the safe side.
