= **__What is this theme used for?__**

= This theme represents the Group of Interest [[[saphir-centre|SAPPHIRE]]] from the **[[[https://fondationscp.wikidot.com/|French SCP Branch]]]**.

= For further information:

  [[div class="blockquote"]]
The **Society of the Atheist Partisans of Progress for the Halt of the Irrational and Religious Enemy**, better known as "**SAPPHIRE**" ("SAPHIR" in French, //La Société Athée Pour la Halte de l'Idéologie Religieuse//), is a Group of Interest most established in Western Europe, but also present in Eastern countries, Northern Africa, and the East Coast of the United States.

The oldest known actions linked to SAPPHIRE date back to 1909, although the organization remained obscure until the 1960s, when SAPPHIRE seemingly went through a major expansion. The precise cause for this is unknown.

SAPPHIRE opposes all forms of belief, religion, and superstition, which they regard as the underlying cause of the woes affecting mankind, as well as SCP objects, which they consider to be the source of the aforementioned beliefs, religions, and superstitions.

In spite of the ideal of rationality and humanism SAPPHIRE claims to adhere to, they do not hesitate to use anomalous means for terrorist purposes, committing attacks against both traditional and occult religions, the primary victims of these attacks often being civilians. Attacks can also be directed against superstitions, targeting [[[scp-227-fr|civilian clairvoyance enthusiasts]]], or rival organizations (including the Foundation).

The SAPPHIRE Format is known in-universe as the “Zetetic Bulletin”.[[footnote]]Fun fact! The Flat Earth Society was originally known as the Universal Zetetic Society. [[/footnote]] For an example of the format in action, see:

* **[[[zetetic-bulletin-wu-xing-iris|The Myth of the Wu Xing Iris]]]** by **[[*user Uncle Nicolini]]**.

Open the format by pasting the following div //before// or //after// the rating module:[[footnote]] If you ask me, it looks cleaner //before//. [[/footnote]]

[[div class="code slim"]]
@@[[div class="sapphire-block"]]@@
[[/div]]

The SAPPHIRE Format is a mock essay or dissertation. Each paragraph is **indented**[[footnote]] Except for the introductory paragraph. [[/footnote]] and the entire format's text is **justified**. Both these properties are pre-baked into the theme's CSS.

Next, indicate the format's title and in-universe author's name (not your own):

[[div class="code slim"]]
@@++* Title@@
@@+++* By Name@@
[[/div]]

Then, a section heading, which is often used several times throughout the format:

[[div class="code slim"]]
@@[[div class="lite-heading"]]@@
@@----@@
@@++++ SECTION HEADING@@
@@----@@
@@[[/div]]@@
[[/div]]

For quoted passages:

[[div class="code slim"]]
@@[[div class="excerpt"]]@@
Text here.
@@[[/div]]@@
[[/div]]

Example:

[[div class="excerpt"]]
Ignorance more frequently begets confidence than does knowledge: it is those who know little, and not those who know much, who so positively assert that this or that problem will never be solved by science.[((bibcite darwin))]
[[/div]]

If your article has any footnotes or bibliographic citations, place their respective blocks at the end of all inner elements. If you're unsure about how to set up a bibliography, [*https://www.wikidot.com/doc-wiki-syntax:bibliography Wikidot provides a short explanation]. Any bibliography section should come after the footnotes:

[[div class="code slim"]]
@@~~~~@@
@@[[footnoteblock]]@@
[[/div]]

Finally, close the format. Include SAPPHIRE's watchword for extra points:

[[div class="code slim"]]
@@----@@
@@+++* When in doubt, doubt.@@
@@[[/div]]@@
[[/div]]

And you're done!
----
