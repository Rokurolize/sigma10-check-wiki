This is a theme based on [https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/theme:xmas the xmas theme] originally created by [[*user Dc_Yerko]] for the [[[http://lafundacionscp.wikidot.com|Spanish Translation Site]]] and adapted by [[*user stormbreath]]. Code for the snow flakes adapted by [[*user EstrellaYoshte]].

Modified by [[*user Mooagain]] to be more fitting for Halloween, also drew the spider image.

Use it on your own article with the following:

[[div class="code"]]
@@[[include :scp-wiki:theme:spidersfalling@@
@@|spidersfalling-spiders= --]]]@@
[[/div]]


Likewise, to use with Black Highlighter:

[[div class="code"]]
@@[[include :scp-wiki:theme:black-highlighter-theme]]@@
@@[[include :scp-wiki:theme:spidersfalling@@
@@|spidersfalling-spiders-bhl= --]]]@@
[[/div]]
