This is the Diamond Highlighter Theme by [[*user tetsusquared]].

This theme extends [[[theme:black-highlighter-theme|Black Highlighter]]] and makes minimal changes. It is primarily intended to standardize the [[[secure-facility-dossier-site-418|Minecraft Division]]] theme between pages[[footnote]] even though only 6468 and Site-418 use it, but there'll be more I PROMISE [[/footnote]].

The header will automatically be translated on the **CN**, **FR**, **JP**, **KO**, **PL**, **VN** and **ZH**[[footnote]]To keep things consistent between CN and ZH, the wording from the CN translation of the Site-418 dossier is used as the standard.[[/footnote]] wikis, as those are the wikis on which at least one Minecraft Division translation exists. If you are from a branch not listed and translate an article from the Minecraft Division, please send me ([[*user tetsusquared]]) a Wikidot PM with a link to the article so I can "officialize" your translation of the theme. In the meantime, you may override the {{header-title}} and {{header-subtitle}} parameters until I get to it.

If you translate this theme, please send me ([[*user tetsusquared]]) a Wikidot PM so I can keep you updated on updates to the theme. Including this page on the EN wiki is preferred so I can roll out changes to all wikis at once.

To use this theme, please put the following at the top of your page:

[[div class="code"]]
@@[[include :scp-wiki:theme:black-highlighter-theme]]@@
@@[[include :scp-wiki:theme:diamond-highlighter]]@@
[[/div]]
