For those without the patience to indulge my metafictional inclinations **Greystyle** is a visual theme created by [[*user Greyve]] for use in his articles. It is essentially a light variant of  [[*user Woedenaz]]'s [*https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/theme:penumbra-bhl Penumbra BHL] with several personal stylistic changes.

**Note that Greystyle is incompatible with Sigma-9 and Basalt.**
