[[div class="tessera-body"]]
This is a theme created for the [https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/tales-of-the-mosaic Tales of the Mosaic] series.

It's based on the [https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/theme:black-highlighter-theme Black Highlighter Theme] and uses the toggle sidebar and centered header components. 

This theme is solely intended for writing Tesserae (stories in the series). You can use it for other things, but at your own risk.

To use this theme, put the following at the top of your page:

[[div class="code"]]
@@[[include :scp-wiki:theme:black-highlighter-theme]]@@
@@[[include :scp-wiki:component:toggle-sidebar-bhl]]@@
@@[[include :scp-wiki:component:centered-header-bhl]]@@
@@[[include :scp-wiki:theme:tales-of-the-mosaic]]@@
[[/div]]

The theme contains three "time of day" scenes for the header image. Day is active by default. To switch to dusk, swap out the last line of the code above for this one:

[[div class="code"]]
@@[[include :scp-wiki:theme:tales-of-the-mosaic | dusk=--]]@@
[[/div]]

For night, use this one:

[[div class="code"]]
@@[[include :scp-wiki:theme:tales-of-the-mosaic | night=--]]@@
[[/div]]

[[/div]]

[[div class="code"]]

@@[[/div]]@@

@@[[/div]]@@

@@[[div class="earthworm"]]@@
@@[[div class="tessera-eyebrow-container"]]@@
@@[[span class="tessera-eyebrow-text"]]Previous[[/span]]@@
@@[[/div]]@@**PREVIOUS-TALE**

@@[[div class="tessera-eyebrow-container"]]@@
@@[[span class="tessera-eyebrow-text"]]Next[[/span]]@@
@@[[/div]]@@**NEXT-TALE**
@@[[/div]]@@
[[/div]]

All you need to do is replace the **ALL CAPS** text in that template with your content. Here's more info about what to put there:

|| **TITLE** || Put the title of your story here.  ||
|| **YOUR-NAME** || Your username. If you have an authorpage, you can link to it. If you're using someone's art, you can put "art by" on a new line under this, and include the name of the artist. ||
|| **STORY-GOES-HERE** || This is your story. If you're not sure what to put in this section, you may not be ready to post a Tessera today. ||
|| **PREVIOUS-TALE**|| This should be a link to the previous Tessera. You should write this as @@[[[TITLE OF TESSERA]]]@@. ||
|| **NEXT-TALE**|| This is a link to the next Tessera. If there isn't one yet, put @@//Coming soon//@@ here instead. ||

Remember to add a preview component and the licensebox component to your page, too. You should also add [https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/forum/t-17022412/should-your-article-have-a-2nd-rating-module Croquembouche's AB Test] to the bottom, because Jezixo considers it good luck to do so.

**Audio instructions**
To create a Tessera with audio, make a page with the template below. 

Upload the audio file to your page, and get the direct link to it (by clicking "Files" at the bottom of the page, then "Info", and copying the link next to "Full file URL"). 

Then, paste that link into the template, replacing "**AUDIO-FILE-LINK**". 

If someone else made the audio, don't forget to credit them in the byline section.
