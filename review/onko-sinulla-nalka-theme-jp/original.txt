++++ Wherever you are.

A theme for Onko Sinulla Nälkä; recipe blog for Nälkäns of all shapes and sizes around the globe to contribute to!

This //can// also be used for general sarkic pages... but I'd recommend changing the horizontal rule and sidebar image. They both say "Onko Sinulla Nälkä" on them. Might also want to change the fonts while you're at it. These are very soft and cutesy. Unless that's the vibe you're going for... Either way.

Come! Sit and join the meal with us.

+ Syntax Examples

Titles can be created by putting between one and six plus "+" at the start of the line

[[collapsible show="+ Titles" hide="- Titles"]]
+ First Title

++ Second Title

+++ Third Title

++++ Fourth Title

+++++ Fifth Title

++++++ Sixth Title
[[/collapsible]]

A horizontal rule can be created with 5 hyphens "@@-----@@" and extends across the whole page if it's not placed inside anything (e.g. a blockquote). The lines separating sections of this document are horizontal rules.
