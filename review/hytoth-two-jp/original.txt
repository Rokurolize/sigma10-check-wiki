This is the Second Second Hytoth Theme by [[*user badooga]]. It is otherwise known as the Second Theme for the Second Hytoth—or, in formal contexts, Second Hytoth Theme the Second.

Let's try that again. This is a [[[theme:basalt|Basalt]]]-based theme for the [[[second-hytoth-hub|Church of the Second Hytoth]]] group of interest! It is a modernization of the [[[theme:hytoth|Sigma-9]]] theme by [[*user macro_au_micro]] and [[*user NatVoltaic]] that also adds plenty of other bells and whistles.

I also grabbed a decent bit of CSS from the [[[theme:foxtrot|Foxtrot]]] theme by [[*user Liryn]] and [[[SCP-9628]]] by [[*user sprawlingstar]]. Check them both out!
